About Us

Ajmal Super 40 is an integrated coaching institute in Hojai, Assam, set up in 2016 under the Ajmal Foundation. It prepares students from Northeast India for NEET-UG and IIT-JEE, and supports meritorious students, especially those from economically weaker backgrounds.

Our Mission

To support and guide meritorious students, especially those from economically weaker backgrounds, so that they can succeed in NEET-UG and IIT-JEE.

Courses & Facilities

Courses

  1. One-Year Repeaters Course (for Class 12 pass-outs)
  2. Two-Year Integrated Program (after Class 10)
  3. Four and Five-Year Foundation Programs (after Class 8 or 7)
